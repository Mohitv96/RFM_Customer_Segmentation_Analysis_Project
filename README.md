## 📋 Project Description

This project performs a comprehensive **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis** 
on e-commerce transaction data to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior. 
The goal is to identify high-value customers, detect at-risk segments, and provide 
actionable insights for targeted marketing strategies.

### 🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses face challenges in:
- Identifying their most valuable customers
- Detecting customers at risk of churning
- Optimizing marketing budgets across customer segments
- Understanding seasonal purchasing patterns
- Making data-driven retention decisions

### 💡 Solution Approach

Using **RFM methodology**, I segmented **2,500+ customers** into **8 distinct groups** 
based on their:
- **Recency:** When they last purchased
- **Frequency:** How often they purchase
- **Monetary:** How much they spend

This segmentation enables targeted marketing strategies and helps prioritize 
retention efforts on high-value customers.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. 🏆 The Pareto Principle Validated
- **Champions segment (27% of customers) generated 65% of total revenue (₹11.78 Crores)**
- A perfect demonstration of the 80/20 rule in action
- **Recommendation:** Focus 70% of retention budget on Champions

### 2. 📈 Massive Seasonal Spike Discovered
- November-December revenue: **₹37 Million per month**
- 3x higher than average months (₹9-12 Million)
- Aligned with Indian festive season (Diwali, Christmas, New Year)
- **Recommendation:** Allocate 40% of annual marketing budget to Q4

### 3. ⚠️ Significant Churn Risk Identified
- **1,092 customers (44%) classified as at-risk:**
  - Hibernating: 645 customers
  - Lost: 447 customers
- Combined revenue impact: ₹2.5 Crores
- **Recommendation:** Launch win-back campaigns immediately

### 4. 🎯 Top Customer Profile
- **CUST01455 (Uttar Pradesh):** ₹7.82 Lakhs spent, 69 orders
- Top 10 customers contribute ₹70 Lakhs to revenue
- **Recommendation:** Implement VIP customer program

### 5. 🗺️ Geographic Revenue Leaders
